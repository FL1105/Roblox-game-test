PRE-REGISTRATION: Kalshi 15-minute BTC up/down, "trade the last 2.5 to 2 minutes"
Written 2026-10-20, before any Kalshi price, any BTC spot price or any market result was read.
Do not edit after results exist. A change of plan goes in a new dated file, and the old one stays.

QUESTION
Does buying the trending side of KXBTC15M between 12:30 and 13:00 into the 15-minute window (2:30 to 2:00 left)
make money after the spread and Kalshi's fee? And does a gap-versus-volatility model, or a bias in how cheap
and expensive contracts are priced, do better than the plain trend rule?

UNIVERSE
Every settled KXBTC15M market whose close time falls in the last 180 days (or as far back as the API serves, if
less). The newest 20 minutes are excluded so every market has settled. Markets with no quote at the entry minute,
a missing side, or a bid/ask spread above 10 cents are skipped and counted, never filled in.

ENTRY AND FILL (identical for every strategy)
- Data is 1-minute candles, so the entry is the quote at the close of the candle ending 120 s before close (the
  13:00 mark, 2:00 left, the end of the user's window). 12:30 cannot be observed with 1-minute data.
- Buying YES costs the YES ask. Buying NO costs 100 minus the YES bid. One order of 10 contracts, taker.
- Fee: ceil(0.07 * multiplier * C * P * (1 - P) to the next cent) for the whole order, divided back per contract.
  The multiplier is read from the series object. P&L per contract = payoff (100 or 0) - price - fee, in cents.
- The result is Kalshi's own recorded result, never inferred from BTC data.

SPOT DATA
Coinbase BTC-USD 1-minute candles. Trend is measured against the Coinbase price at the window start, not against
Kalshi's strike, so a constant exchange-versus-index offset cannot leak in. The offset is reported as a diagnostic.

THE FIVE PRE-REGISTERED STRATEGIES (the only ones that get a verdict)
1. trend_open    YES if spot at entry > spot at window start, NO if lower. (The user's rule.)
2. trend_mom3    YES if spot at entry > spot 3 minutes earlier, NO if lower.
3. trend_kalshi  YES if the YES mid-price at entry > its value 3 minutes earlier, NO if lower.
4. model_edge    p_up = Phi(gap / (sigma * sqrt(tau_eff))), gap = ln(spot_entry / spot_start), sigma = std of the
                 trailing 60 one-minute log returns up to the entry (per-minute), tau_eff = minutes left - 2/3
                 (the market settles on a 60-second average, which cuts the variance of the final value).
                 Buy the side with the larger edge (model probability minus price minus fee), only if it is at
                 least 3 cents. The 3 cents is fixed in advance and not tuned.
5. favorite      Buy whichever side has the higher price (YES mid above 50 -> YES, below -> NO).

CONTROLS (reported, no verdict): always_yes, always_no. They show the fee and spread drag and the base rate.

TIME SPLIT
Markets are ordered by close time. The first 60% are in-sample, the last 40% out-of-sample. Nothing is fitted, so
the split is only there to stop me from reading a result off the same data I looked at while designing the report.

DECISION RULE
A strategy is declared to have an edge only if ALL hold:
 (a) out-of-sample trades >= 300;
 (b) out-of-sample mean P&L per contract > 0 with one-sided p < 0.01 (0.05 divided by the 5 strategies);
 (c) in-sample mean P&L per contract is also > 0.
Otherwise the verdict is "no edge shown", whatever the point estimate looks like.

EXPLORATORY (labelled as such, no verdicts, may not be used to rescue a failed strategy)
- Win rate and P&L by price bucket for both sides (the favorite-longshot check).
- model_edge with thresholds 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 cents.
- Entry 180, 240 and 300 s before close.
- Brier score of the model, the market mid-price and a 50/50 blend.
- Losing-streak and win/loss size statistics, because the plain trend rule wins often and loses big.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
Fills are assumed at the quoted best price for 10 contracts with no queue or depth data. Candle quotes are the
close of each minute, not the moment of the order. The 60-second settlement average is from memory and is
checked against the market's rules text by `python -m kalshi_btc15 probe`.
