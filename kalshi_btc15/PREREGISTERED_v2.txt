PRE-REGISTRATION v2: a stacked model that uses the market price as its base
Written 2026-10-21, after Report 1 (preliminary) and before any v2 model was fitted or any holdout market was downloaded.
Report 1 showed that the gap/volatility model of PREREGISTERED.txt is worse than the market's own mid-price
(Brier 0.0773 vs 0.0722) and loses money; all five v1 strategies showed no edge. This file is a NEW hypothesis,
decided with Report 1 in view, so it is tested only on data Report 1 never touched.

QUESTION
Do the Coinbase gap, the 3-minute market momentum, the last-trade-versus-mid imbalance and the 3-minute BTC momentum
add information to the market's own price at 2 minutes left? And if so, enough to beat the spread and fee?

DATA SPLIT (fixed by date, before any holdout data is read)
- DEV (training; Report 1 already saw it): close time in [2025-12-10, 2026-01-04) or [2026-08-21, now), restricted to
  the markets already downloaded.
- HOLDOUT (never seen): settled markets with close time in [2026-01-04, 2026-08-21). A deterministic sample: sort that
  range by close time and take every 4th market (position % 4 == 0). Sampling uses no prices or outcomes.
- The model is fitted once on DEV and frozen. The holdout is scored once. Nothing is re-fitted or tuned after seeing it.

ENTRY, FILLS, FEES: identical to PREREGISTERED.txt and Amendments 1 and 2 (quote at the close of the candle ending
120 s before close; buy at the ask; 10 contracts taker; quadratic fee; the market's recorded result).

MODEL: logistic regression, probability that the market resolves YES
  logit(p) = b0 + b1*x1 + b2*x2 + b3*x3 + b4*x4 + b5*x5
  x1 = logit(clip(mid/100, 0.005, 0.995))                          the market (b1 = 1, b0 = 0 would mean the market is calibrated)
  x2 = clip(ln(spot_entry/spot_open) / (sigma * sqrt(tau_eff)), -6, 6)   the v1 gap z-score (same sigma and tau_eff as v1)
  x3 = x1 - logit(clip(mid_3min_earlier/100, 0.005, 0.995))       market momentum
  x4 = (last_trade - mid) / 100, 0 if no trade in that minute      aggressor side
  x5 = clip(ln(spot_entry/spot_3min_earlier) / (sigma * sqrt(3)), -6, 6)   BTC momentum in sigma units
x2..x5 are standardised with the DEV mean and standard deviation. Ridge penalty lambda = 1.0 on b2..b5 only (b0, b1 free).
Fitted by Newton / IRLS on DEV. No feature selection, no lambda search, no other variants: this is the only model.
Markets missing any input (no BTC data, no earlier quote) are excluded and counted.

PRE-REGISTERED TESTS (on HOLDOUT, once)
 H_F (forecast)  Mean per-market log loss of the stacked model is lower than that of the raw mid-price. Paired difference,
                 one-sided normal p; pass at p < 0.01. Also reported against a calibration-only model (x1 alone, fitted on
                 DEV), so calibration and new information can be told apart. That comparison is secondary, no verdict.
 H_T (trading)   stack_edge: compute p from the model; for each side, edge = 100*p_side - price - fee (cents per contract);
                 buy the side with the larger edge if it is at least 2.0 cents. Holdout trades >= 300, mean P&L per contract
                 > 0 with one-sided p < 0.01, and the DEV in-sample mean P&L also > 0. Otherwise "no edge shown".
Both are reported whatever happens. H_F can pass while H_T fails (information too small to pay the spread and fee);
that outcome is the expected one for an efficient market and is a legitimate finding.

ALSO REPORTED (no verdict): fitted coefficients with standard errors; the five v1 strategies on the holdout; the
stack_edge threshold at 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 cents; holdout P&L by calendar month.

FINAL v1 VERDICT (per Amendment 2): one run of the v1 report on DEV plus the sampled HOLDOUT, with the unchanged 60/40
time split and decision rule. It is run once after the holdout is downloaded.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
The model is fitted on data that includes August to October 2026 and scored on January to August 2026, so it is not a
walk-forward test; a pass would still need a forward test before any real use. The holdout is a 1-in-4 sample, which
reduces power: at ~5,500 markets the smallest detectable edge for a trend-type trade is roughly 1.5 cents per contract.
