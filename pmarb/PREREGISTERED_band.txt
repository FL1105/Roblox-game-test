PRE-REGISTRATION: "selling the band" between Kalshi's and Polymarket's BTC 15-minute start references
Written 2026-10-22, before any historical hedge was scored against outcomes.

IDEA
Kalshi (60 s BRTI average) and Polymarket (Chainlink TWAP) measure "up" from slightly different start levels. A hedge
that buys YES on one venue and NO on the other pays $0 only when the price ends BETWEEN the two references, $2 when it ends
between them the other way round, and $1 otherwise. If the venues' quoted price difference overstates how often the price
really lands in that band, the hedge has positive expected value. The live gaps (mean 5c, up to 27c) look large next to the
1.33% historical rate at which the two venues' final outcomes disagree.

DATA (fixed before scoring)
The 1,200 randomly drawn windows already collected (Aug 29 - Oct 20, 2026) with both venues' recorded outcomes. Kalshi bid/ask
from the cached 1-minute candle ending 300 s before close. Polymarket Up price = the last price-history point at or before
close - 300 s and no older than 150 s; the book is not available historically, so bid/ask = that price -/+ a half-spread of
0.5c (primary) and 1.0c (sensitivity). Windows with a missing quote on either venue, or a mid outside 3-97c, are skipped and counted.

RULE (no tuning)
At close - 300 s, for each orientation (YES on Kalshi + NO on Polymarket, or YES on Polymarket + NO on Kalshi) compute
net per set = 1 - cost - fees, taker on both legs (Kalshi 0.07 p(1-p), Polymarket crypto 0.07 p(1-p)). Take the better
orientation if its net is at least 2c; otherwise no trade. One set per window.
Realised payoff = [YES leg's venue resolved Up] + [NO leg's venue resolved Down].

TEST
Primary: mean realised net per set over taken trades, one-sided p for mean > 0 (normal approximation), requiring at least
100 taken trades. Reported with the 95% interval, the counts of payoffs 0 / 1 / 2, and the same at half-spread 1.0c.
Also reported (no verdict): how the result varies with the modeled net threshold (0, 2, 5, 10c).
Decision: an edge is claimed only if the primary mean is > 0 with p < 0.01 AND it is still > 0 at the 1.0c half-spread.

LIMITATIONS
Polymarket's historical price is a last/mid price, not a book, so spread and depth are assumed. Fills at size are untested.
The 5-minute entry is one choice among many; it is fixed here and not searched.
