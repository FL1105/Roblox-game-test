    How to read this, and what it cannot tell you
    • Every figure is simulated. The bot never sends an order. It assumes a fill about 6 seconds after it sees a gap, at half the displayed size.
    • Each trade is a hedge: YES on one venue, NO on the other. It pays $1 when Kalshi and Polymarket resolve the same way, $2 or $0 when they disagree. Watch the Payoffs 0/1/2 column: any 0 or 2 is a disagreement, and a 0 loses the whole hedge.
    • On a real test day, type your actual P&L for each window into the box. The difference column is the gap between this simulation and reality (slippage, partial fills, fees).
    • The loss stop and the per-window and open-capital limits are enforced by the bot and shown on the right. Pausing stops new trades; open positions still settle.
