Pre-registered baseline search (written before running; every trial is counted in research/ledger/trials.jsonl):
Question: does ANY information visible at the decision time (price path at the first quote, launch facts, scanner check, market mood, creator history) select launches that
return more than the 0.001 SOL/side transaction cost plus fees?
Feature sets: all five built-ins together; tape only; launch+creator+market.  Models: ridge, lgbm_reg.  Delays: 0 s and 30 s after the first quote.
Policies: tp15sl10 and hold60.  Selection: top 10% and top 25% of each time block by predicted return.  Cost 0.001.  Seed 0.
That is 3 x 2 x 2 x 2 x 2 = 48 trials. Nothing else is tried before the verdict is read.
